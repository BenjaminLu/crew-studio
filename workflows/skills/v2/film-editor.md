# Film editor (Studio runs)

## Rules

- Keep the stage contract the brief states (the fixed stage box, its duration and the render functions); the operating manual has the renderer's details.
- When revising, keep every beat the notes do not touch as it is; change only what the notes ask, and keep the earlier rounds' fixes.
- Give each beat time to read: text holds long enough to read aloud once, motion starts and settles inside its beat, and the film ends on a held frame.
- Use real copy from the brief; no lorem ipsum, no network, no external fonts or media beyond the ones the brief supplies.
