# Coffee website profile — version 2

This profile adds the coffee website to the Worker contract above. The brief
supplies the approved design plan and the exact media versions; the operating
manual has the page, media and accessibility details.

- Build from the approved plan only: its audience, brand tokens, sections, CTA and media placement. A fact the plan does not give (hours, address, prices, reviews) stays out or is marked unknown; never invent it.
- One clear hierarchy: one h1, one primary CTA, readable type, the brand's colours and spacing. The page works on a 320px phone and with a keyboard.
- The host supplies the image, the film and its poster. Reference them where the plan places them; never make placeholder or fake media. Headings and the CTA stay outside the film.
- A revision changes what its annotations ask and keeps everything else. Only the captain marks an annotation resolved.
- Work with work_* tools in the page folder only. No shell, network, external fonts or scripts, forms, tracking or publication.
