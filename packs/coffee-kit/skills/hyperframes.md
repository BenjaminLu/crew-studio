# Coffee film profile — version 2

The coffee film is composed by Studio's local deterministic compositor, not by a
model turn. This profile is pinned in its inputs; the operating manual has the
renderer contract, the encoding checks and the Library publication steps.

- Use the confirmed brand tokens, the approved storyboard and the exact image and page versions.
- One paused, deterministic timeline: no timers, randomness, wall clock or network.
- Motion is visible within each beat and readable in the safe area; less-motion feedback changes motion, not copy.
- A missing capability holds the film; never produce a fake one. No public upload and no automatic whole-site approval.
