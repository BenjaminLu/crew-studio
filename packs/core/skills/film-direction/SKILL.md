# Film direction

- Know the one thing the viewer should remember. Every beat serves it; cut the rest.
- One idea per beat. A beat lasts long enough to read its words twice, and no longer.
- Open on the strongest image within the first two seconds. End on the action or message, not a fade to nothing.
- Vary rhythm: hold on what matters, cut quickly through setup.
- Captions carry the story with the sound off: short lines, high contrast, inside the safe area, timed to the beat.
- Motion has a reason (reveal, emphasis, transition); keep it consistent across scenes.
- Check the rendered film, not the plan: timing, legibility and the first and last frames.
