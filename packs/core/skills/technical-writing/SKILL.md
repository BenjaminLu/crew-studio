# Technical writing

- Name the reader and the task they came to do. Lead with that task, not with background.
- Give exact commands, paths, values and expected output, in code formatting, so they can be copied.
- One idea per paragraph; one step per numbered line. Steps are in the order the reader does them.
- Say what success looks like and what to do when it does not happen.
- State limits and prerequisites before the step that needs them.
- Cut what the reader already knows, history nobody needs, and claims you cannot back.
- Keep one term for one thing throughout.
- Update the doc in the same change as the behaviour it describes.
